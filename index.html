Data processing.

Pre-launch · 8 November 2026. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Account emails are sent through Resend. The sampler beta is available to every signed-in email or Discord account. Song Studio and APK downloads remain paused. Account deletion can be requested in your profile with a 30-day waiting period. Cloud project sync is not active yet.

The browser sampler beta is public and works without an account. The optional account and Discord features remain separate. Song Studio stays locked.

1. Controller and contact

Yorick Arlt is responsible for this website. Full address and contact details are provided below. For privacy requests, contact zynora-studio@outlook.de.

2. Account and profile

Supabase Auth processes your email address, one-time verification codes and session data to provide your account. You do not create an account password for email sign-in. Your unique username and display name are stored in the NEXORA STUDIO project in Frankfurt. One-time codes are valid only briefly and are not displayed in profiles. Profile picture uploads are not available. This website currently displays only your own profile.

3. Necessary storage on your device

The website stores your sign-in session, language preference and acknowledgement of the storage notice in your browser. When you use the sampler, IndexedDB stores named projects, patterns, tempo and imported audio locally under a browser guest ID or your account ID. Autosave runs after changes. These projects are stored only in this browser on this device: they do not sync with Android, another device or a different domain. Export project backups before changing domains or clearing browser data. Signing out does not erase local projects.

There are no advertising or analytics trackers. The storage used for the account and sampler is necessary for the functions you request (§ 25(2)(2) TDDDG); the storage notice is an acknowledgement, not consent to tracking. You may cancel and continue browsing public pages. Delete local projects with the project deletion button or by clearing this website’s browser data.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Account and profile data are processed to provide the service you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Necessary security and operational processing is based on the legitimate interest in secure operation (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Optional public sharing and a marketplace are not active. Imported samples are decoded locally; WAV export and project backup create local files.

5. Hosting, downloads and email

GitHub Pages hosts the public website and processes connection information such as IP address, request time and requested resource for delivery and security. Supabase provides authentication and profiles in the NEXORA STUDIO project. New profile picture uploads are disabled. APK downloads remain disabled.

Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) sends account confirmation, sign-in and password-reset emails on our behalf. This requires the recipient address, the necessary message content including verification links or codes, and technical delivery data. The sender is support@zynora-studio.com; replies are directed to zynora-studio@outlook.de. This sending address does not provide a separate support inbox. Email processing serves the requested account function and secure operation (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Ireland is selected as the sending region. Resend also describes processing in the United States in its privacy policy; the selected sending region is not a guarantee that all processing stays within the EEA. Email open and click tracking are not enabled for this domain. Do not forward confirmation links or codes to other people. Resend privacy policy.

6. Language selection

You can choose the website language manually using the language menu. Your language choice is stored locally in this browser.

7. Retention, deletion and your rights

You can request account deletion under My profile. A 30-day waiting period is shown. During this period, profile edits, project changes and uploads are blocked; you can still sign in to cancel deletion. After the deadline, an hourly cleanup removes your account, profile, uploaded files and associated project data from active systems. Failed deletions are retried. Exported files and local browser copies on your devices are not removed by this process. Technical logs and backups follow the respective providers’ retention periods; legal retention duties remain unaffected. You may also contact us by email for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection where applicable. You may complain to a competent data protection authority, including the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

8. Discord community, support and applications

When you create a new website account with Discord, our bot posts an English registration notification in the designated channel of our Discord server. It contains your Discord mention, Discord ID and the creation time of your website account. Email addresses, passwords and IP addresses are not included. People with access to that Discord channel can see the notification. Signing in again or connecting Discord to an existing email account does not create another registration announcement. Supabase and BotGhost process the necessary identifiers and delivery information for this workflow. Undelivered notifications are retained during an outage for a later attempt; delivery logs are removed after seven days. The Discord message reference is retained so the bot can request removal of the notification when your account is permanently deleted. Removal happens on the next successful bot run and requires the necessary Discord permissions. Email registration remains available as an alternative.

When you choose to sign in with Discord, Supabase receives your Discord ID, profile information and associated email address. Sign-in requests only the identify and email scopes; it does not request permission to read your messages, manage servers or add the bot. Authentication takes place through Discord and Supabase; you do not enter your Discord password on this website. An existing confirmed email address is associated with the same account. You then choose your unique ZYNORA username in your profile. Processing provides the sign-in method you requested (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Email sign-in remains available.

Discord is linked using ordinary external links; no Discord widget is embedded. Clicking a link opens Discord, where Discord processes connection and account information under its own privacy policy. You may need to join the server before a channel becomes accessible. Discord privacy policy.

When you contact our team through a support or application ticket, we process your Discord username, messages and any files you choose to share to handle your request or application. Tickets are visible to the team members and server bots authorized for that channel. Share only what is needed; never include passwords or verification codes. For deletion requests and questions about ticket retention, contact the website owner below.

We handle support to provide the requested service or respond to your enquiry (Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR, depending on the request). Community participation is optional. Do not use Discord for urgent legal or privacy requests; the contact email below is also available.

9. Storage notice and settings

The banner informs you about necessary storage. It does not enable optional tracking. Its acknowledgement is stored as zynora-cookie-notice-v1 in localStorage until you clear browser data. Language and sign-in settings also use localStorage. Sampler projects use IndexedDB when the studio is available. You can reopen the notice using Cookies & storage in the footer. No optional analytics or advertising cookies are set by this website.
